## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of architectural minimalism, industrial design precision, and high-fashion editorial restraint. Engineered for an elevated mobile accessories brand, it evokes tactile desire, engineered protection, and understated luxury. 

The aesthetic is anchored in:
- **Purity of Surface:** Vast expanses of clean, warm-tinted off-white canvases (`#FBFBFD`) juxtaposed against deep obsidian typography (`#0D0D11`).
- **Engineered Restraint:** Avoidance of decorative clutter; visual weight is communicated through meticulous spatial cadence, precise micro-details (hairline dividers, controlled radii), and studio-grade product photography.
- **Micro-tactility:** Interactive states feel deliberate and weighted, simulating the physical sensation of snapping a magnetic case or clicking an anodized aluminum button.

## Colors

The palette is tuned to spotlight physical material finishes—matte silicone, vegetable-tanned leather, woven aramid fiber, and brushed titanium.

- **Primary (`#0D0D11`):** Deep obsidian black for primary actions, structural headlines, and high-impact micro-elements.
- **Secondary (`#8A857D`):** Warm slate-stone gray, utilized for technical specs, secondary buttons, and subdued metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#D8C7B5`):** Soft travertine accent used sparingly for limited edition tags, luxury callouts, or subtle indicator glows.
- **Neutral (`#1A1A1E`):** Charcoal body text ensuring optical comfort without the harsh contrast of pure black.
- **Canvas System:** Surfaces layer from `#FBFBFD` (base background) into `#FFFFFF` (elevated cards and modals) bound by hairline boundaries in `#E8E8ED`.
- **System States:** Success (Forest `#22593D`), Warning (Amber `#9E6721`), and Destructive (Crimson `#A82222`) are muted and desaturated to preserve an editorial atmosphere.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes proportional balance, metric consistency, and modern structural elegance.

- **Primary Family:** Plus Jakarta Sans is deployed across all levels, utilizing precise tracking to shift character between editorial grandeur and utilitarian product clarity.
- **Headlines:** Display and Section Titles use negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`600` / `700`) to create strong contrast with empty white surfaces.
- **Technical Badges:** Feature tracking at `+0.08em` set in `UPPERCASE` with weight `700` for feature markers (e.g., `MAGSAFE READY`, `10FT DROP TESTED`).
- **Tabular Figures & Price Display:** All numeric values in commerce grids and checkout states use proportional or tabular figure alignments to preserve baseline stability when prices update.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column grid system paired with strict 8pt micro-spacing increments.

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max content boundary of `1440px`, centered with `margin` of `3rem` (48px) and `gutter` of `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with `margin` of `2rem` (32px) and `gutter` of `1rem` (16px).
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile` of `1rem` (16px) and `gutter-mobile` of `0.75rem` (12px). Sticky action bars span edge-to-edge with safe-area padding.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Micro-gaps inside components strictly scale using `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), `space-lg` (24px), and `space-xl` (40px). Product grids maintain airy vertical separation between rows to mimic boutique showroom display tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an interaction of surface contrast, hairline borders, and physical-world ambient diffusion. Heavy black drop shadows are prohibited.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FBFBFD` with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Product Cards & Content Tiles):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px border in `#E8E8ED`. Shadow is subtle and diffused: `0px 2px 8px rgba(13, 13, 17, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Subtle lift applied on desktop hover: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 13, 17, 0.06), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 13, 17, 0.03)`, keeping the `1px` `#E8E8ED` border intact.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Drawers, Modals):** Translucent backdrop-filtered surfaces (`rgba(251, 251, 253, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and border-bottom `1px solid rgba(232, 232, 237, 0.8)`). 
- **Slide-out Cart & Sheet Elevation:** Accompanied by a gentle ambient veil (`rgba(13, 13, 17, 0.35)`) with an offset shadow: `0px 24px 64px -12px rgba(13, 13, 17, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances the fluid curves of modern consumer electronics with clean industrial architecture.

- **Standard Elements (Level 2):** Inputs, select boxes, and smaller interactive modules take a base radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Product tiles, banners, and structured containers employ `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px) to harmonize with the physical curvature of smartphone hardware.
- **Pills & Badges:** Micro-labels, material selectors, action tags, and status chips always utilize complete circular capsule shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Consistency Rule:** Inner nested elements must always feature a smaller border radius than their parent container, maintaining concentricity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep obsidian `#0D0D11` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.75rem` vertical by `1.5rem` horizontal padding, `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg` (8px). Hover state transitions to `#1A1A1E` with subtle micro-scale (`transform: scale(1.005)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px solid #E8E8ED`, `#0D0D11` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F4F4F7` with border `#D5D5DB`.
- **Sticky Add-to-Cart (Mobile):** Fixed bottom drawer with integrated Apple Pay / checkout flow, full width, high-contrast, utilizing safe-area padding.

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Pill Badges:** Height `24px`, padding `0 10px`, background `#F4F4F7`, text `#0D0D11`, border `1px solid #E8E8ED`. Uses `label-badge` typography in uppercase (e.g., `MAGSAFE`, `10FT DROP TESTED`, `RECYCLED ALUMINUM`).
- **Accent Badges:** For exclusives, uses a soft warm-sand fill `#F5EFEB` with deep stone typography `#665545`.

### Product Cards
- Built on a pure white `#FFFFFF` tile with `1.5rem` (24px) radius and a 1px `#E8E8ED` border.
- Features neutral, low-contrast studio photography with an aspect ratio of `4:5` or `1:1`.
- Secondary hover reveal shows alternate lifestyle view or interior protective engineering.
- Interactive color swatches embedded directly on the card face: 14px circular color rings with a 2px offset border indicating active selection.
- Clear, unencumbered hierarchy: Product Title, Series/Compatibility, Swatches, and Price.

### Color & Material Swatch Pickers
- Concentric circle design: 20px inner color fill, 2px white gap ring, surrounded by an outer 1.5px border of `#0D0D11` when active. Inactive swatches show no outer ring until hovered.
- Micro-tooltip displays color name on cursor focus (e.g., "Obsidian Black", "Sage Leather", "Raw Titanium").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E8ED`, radius `8px`. Focus state introduces a crisp `1px solid #0D0D11` border with zero colored halo rings.
- **Labels:** Floated or positioned outside the input using `body-sm` in `#8A857D` to prevent visual clutter.

### Announcement & Cart Drawer
- **Announcement Bar:** Compact `36px` banner in `#0D0D11` with crisp white tracking text (`11px`, weight `500`), communicating global complimentary shipping or warranty guarantees.
- **Cart Drawer:** Side-sliding overlay with frictionless tier markers (e.g., dynamic "Free Express Shipping" visual progress line). Express pay buttons (Apple Pay, Shop Pay) positioned above standard checkout triggers to maximize mobile conversion.
